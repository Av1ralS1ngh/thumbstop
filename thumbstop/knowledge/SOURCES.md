# Where the knowledge layer comes from

`hooks.md` is the knowledge layer the side-by-side judge reads before a verdict (`thumbstop/judge.py`,
`knowledge=`). It was distilled on 8 Nov 2026 from two research briefs, one on hook psychology and stand-up craft and one on
short-drama industry practice (general sources only, nothing from any show's scripts). Each principle is written as something a judge can check
in a transcript.
